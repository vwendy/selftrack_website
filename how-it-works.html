How it works and your privacy

SelfTrack pairs two things: how you use your phone, and how you say you feel. Here is exactly what it does with each.

What it never records

  • The words you type, passwords, messages or notification contents.
  • What is on your screen, or screenshots.
  • Your location, contacts or clipboard.

Password fields and the lock screen are skipped automatically. Banking, payment and password-manager apps start on an exclusion list, and you can change that list at any time.

Where your data goes

Nowhere, unless you send it. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot upload anything, and we never receive your data. Your data is stored on your phone. You can export it as files through Android's file picker and share it only if you choose to.

The optional tap, scroll and typing tracking

This uses Android's Accessibility permission, which is powerful. Apps that misuse it can read a screen, which is why Android warns you when you turn it on. SelfTrack is set up so it cannot read window contents, and its code never stores text, but the permission itself gives more access than SelfTrack uses. That means you are trusting the app, so only install it from people you trust.

It is optional. Without it, SelfTrack still records app use, screen events, sensors and mood check-ins.

Your controls

  • Stop monitoring with one tap on the Data Collection tab.
  • Pause or withdraw the tap, scroll and typing tracking at any time, and choose apps to exclude.
  • Export your data as files whenever you like.
  • Delete all data on the Data management tab. It erases every check-in and all recorded behavior from the app. Files you already exported stay where you saved them, so delete those yourself.
  • Uninstall the app to remove it entirely.

What the charts mean

SelfTrack plots your own social media time in the hour before each check-in against your Depression and Anxiety scores, and gives a plain-language reading. This shows whether two things move together in your own data. It does not show that one causes the other.

The check-in questions are adapted from the PHQ-2 and GAD-2 screening questions, with the time window shortened to "the past hour or so" and each answer on a 0 to 100 slider. That is not the validated clinical version, so treat the scores as an informal personal signal and not a diagnosis.

Not a medical tool. If you are struggling, please talk to a healthcare professional. In the US you can call or text 988.

Not a research study

SelfTrack is a personal self-tracking tool, not a research study. We do not collect, receive, store or analyze your data, and there is nothing to sign up for. Anything you export stays wherever you save it.

SelfTrack is a self-reflection tool. It is not medical advice. We never receive your data.
