Feedback

Tell us what was confusing, what broke, or what you wish the app did. It helps us improve SelfTrack for everyone.

Open the feedback form

Helpful things to mention

  • Your phone model and Android version (for example "Samsung Galaxy S23, Android 15").
  • Which step or screen you were on.
  • What you expected and what happened instead.
Please don't include personal or sensitive details, such as your name, health information, mood scores, or exported data files. The form does not need any of them.

Feedback is anonymous unless you choose to add an email address so we can reply.

Other ways to reach us

For bugs and feature requests you can also open an issue on GitHub (this needs a GitHub account and is public, so don't include personal details). For anything else, see Contact.

SelfTrack is a self-reflection tool. It is not medical advice. We never receive your data.
