Feedback

Tell us what was confusing, what broke, or what you wish the app did. It helps us improve SelfTrack for everyone.






Please don't include personal or sensitive details, such as your name, health information, mood scores, or exported data files. This form does not need any of them.

Send it from my email app instead

Where your message goes: pressing Send delivers it to admin@chineseartlab.org through FormSubmit, a form-forwarding service, so you don't need an email account or app. The message contains only what you type, so we don't get your name, email or phone details unless you enter them. FormSubmit handles your message and your IP address to deliver it and keeps submissions for about 30 days (see its privacy policy). Nothing is stored on this website.

Other ways to reach us

For bugs and feature requests you can also open an issue on GitHub (this needs a GitHub account and is public, so don't include personal details). For anything else, see Contact.

SelfTrack is a self-reflection tool. It is not medical advice. We never receive your data.
